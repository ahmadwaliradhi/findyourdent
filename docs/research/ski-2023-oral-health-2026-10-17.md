# SKI 2023 oral health figures: verification, 17 Oct 2026

Closes H03 (issue #3). Checks the Survei Kesehatan Indonesia 2023 figures used in the deck, video and spec 01 against the official report. Supersedes the "likely" parts of section 8 of `facts-2026-10-16.md`.

## Sources read

- **SKI 2023 Dalam Angka**, BKPK Kemenkes (965-page PDF, created 21 May 2024). Bab 9 "Kesehatan Gigi dan Mulut" runs from printed p. 317 to p. 347 (PDF pp. 356 to 386; printed page = PDF page − 39). Report page: https://www.badankebijakan.kemkes.go.id/ski-2023-dalam-angka/
- **BKPK factsheet** *Kesehatan Gigi dan Mulut di Indonesia: Hasil Survei Kesehatan Indonesia 2023* (2 pages, © 2024 BKPK Kemenkes): https://repository.badankebijakan.kemkes.go.id/5534/1/04%20factsheet%20Gilut_bahasa.pdf

Each table cell below was read off a rendered image of the page, not only from extracted text.

## Verdict

| Figure as used | Status | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| 56.9% of people aged 3+ had a dental or oral problem in the past 12 months | **Confirmed** | Dalam Angka, Tabel 9.5, p. 324 (95% CI 56.6 to 57.3); factsheet Gambar 1, p. 1 |
| Only 11.2% of them were treated by dental staff | **Confirmed, with a caveat**: it is in the factsheet only, not in any Dalam Angka table | Factsheet Gambar 3, p. 2 ("hanya 11,2% yang berobat ke tenaga medis"); Riskesdas 2018 comparison 10.2% |
| 57.0% of those who did not seek care felt no need | **Confirmed, wording corrected**: the denominator is everyone aged 3+ not treated by a dental health worker, including people with no dental problem | Dalam Angka, Tabel 9.7, p. 327 (95% CI 56.5 to 57.4) |

## Tabel 9.5, p. 324: problem and who treated it (age 3+, last 12 months)

| | Indonesia | Jawa Timur |
|---|---|---|
| Has a dental or oral problem | **56.9%** (N 829,573) | 51.2% (N 124,362) |
| Of those treated: by dentist or dental specialist | 81.4% | 82.4% |
| Of those treated: by dental therapist | 4.5% | 3.3% |
| Of those treated: by GP or other health worker | 17.1% | 16.6% |
| Weighted N of those treated | 58,564 | 8,724 |

The three "of those treated" columns sum to more than 100%, so a person can count under more than one. The 81.4% is a share of *treated* people, not of people with a problem; do not present it as a care rate.

## Tabel 9.7, p. 327: never treated by a dental health worker, and why (age 3+)

| | Indonesia | Jawa Timur |
|---|---|---|
| Never treated by a dental health worker | **91.9%** (N 829,573) | 91.9% (N 124,362) |
| Reason: never had toothache | 61.1% | 66.1% |
| Reason: felt no need | **57.0%** | 57.7% |
| Reason: went to a tukang gigi | 1.1% | 1.1% |
| Reason: self-medicated | 24.8% | 20.7% |
| Weighted N of those giving reasons | 767,244 | 114,991 |

Reasons are multiple-response. The 91.9% uses all interviewees aged 3+ as its denominator, not only those with a problem.

## Caveats

1. **11.2% cannot be reproduced from Dalam Angka.** Using the weighted Ns in Tabel 9.5, people treated by any health worker are 58,564 of about 472,000 with a problem (0.569 × 829,573), or about 12.4%; treated by a dentist or specialist only is about 10.1%. The factsheet does not give its definition. Cite the factsheet for 11.2%, not "Tabel 9.x".
2. **Do not use factsheet Gambar 4** ("Alasan masyarakat tidak mencari pengobatan": Covid-19 81.7%, waiting time 80.2%, self-treatment 79.3%, tukang gigi 77.5%, no money 76.7%, no pain 70.2%). It contradicts Tabel 9.7 (tukang gigi 1.1%, self-medicated 24.8%) and has no stated denominator.
3. **Jawa Timur** (51.2%) is below the national 56.9% and ties with Riau as the 7th-lowest of 38 provinces (Bali 46.5% is lowest, Sulawesi Selatan and Sulawesi Barat 68.4% highest). Use it only if the deck talks about Surabaya; there are no kota or kabupaten figures in this report.
4. Riskesdas 2018 comparison values (57.6% problem, 10.2% treated) come from factsheet Gambar 3.

## Ready-to-use wording

> Survei Kesehatan Indonesia 2023: 56.9% of Indonesians aged 3 and over had a dental or oral problem in the past year (SKI 2023 Dalam Angka, Tabel 9.5), yet only 11.2% of them were treated by dental health workers (BKPK SKI 2023 oral health factsheet). Among people not treated by a dental health worker, 57.0% said they felt no need (Tabel 9.7).

For Jawa Timur: 51.2% with a problem (Tabel 9.5); 57.7% of those not treated felt no need (Tabel 9.7).
