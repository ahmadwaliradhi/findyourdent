## 1. JKN dental benefits (FKTP vs FKRTL, scaling, dentures)

**Answer**
- **FKTP dental package (capitation)**, Permenkes 3/2023 Pasal 3 ayat (2): examination, treatment and consultation; premedication; oro-dental emergencies; extraction of primary teeth (topical/infiltration); extraction of permanent teeth without complications; post-extraction medication; fillings (tumpatan); **scaling for acute gingivitis**. Capitation for a praktik dokter gigi mandiri is Rp3,000 to Rp4,000 per enrollee per month (Pasal 4 huruf d).
- **Needs referral to FKRTL:** anything "spesialistik". The BPJS guide says dental referrals are made only "atas indikasi medis" for specialist or subspecialist examination or treatment. A referral can come only from a dentist, unless the puskesmas or klinik has no dentist. **Root canal (PSA) is not on the FKTP capitation list**, so under JKN it goes through referral to a hospital Sp.KG (likely). Clinically, PNPK 1492/2023 Table 3.3 says a general dentist can do PSA at FKTP. That is a statement about clinical competence, not JKN coverage.
- **Not covered:** aesthetic treatment, orthodontics, and care at non-partner facilities except in an emergency (BPJS guide ch. VI; Perpres 82/2018 Pasal 52).
- **Scaling frequency is conflicting.** The older BPJS guide says "1x dalam setahun" (once a year). Permenkes 3/2023 frames scaling by indication ("gingivitis akut"), and the summary of its text states no frequency. Media citing BPJS social accounts say once every 2 years. Treat it as indication-based and do not promise a frequency.
- **Dentures (protesa gigi):** non-capitation, claimed by the facility (Permenkes 3/2023 Pasal 11 huruf j). FKTP cap per Pasal 24 ayat (2) is **Rp1,000,000 for both jaws, Rp500,000 for one jaw**. Pasal 24 ayat (1) huruf b says the same jaw can be redone "paling cepat 2 (dua) tahun sekali" (at the earliest every 2 years), on medical indication. The older BPJS guide adds a per-jaw breakdown: 1 to 8 teeth Rp250,000, 9 to 16 teeth Rp500,000. Dentures are also available at FKRTL. Pasal 47 has a separate hospital cap that reads as about Rp1.1M, but the extracted text was garbled (unconfirmed).

**Confidence:** FKTP list and denture caps **confirmed** (Permenkes 3/2023 via full-text mirror, plus the BPJS guide). Root canal needing referral **likely**. Scaling frequency **unconfirmed (conflicting)**.

## 2. Can a participant use ANY BPJS dentist? (critical)

**Answer: No.** Dental care under JKN is tied to the participant's registration.
- **Registered at a puskesmas or klinik:** the participant is served by that facility's own dentist or its **jejaring** (network) dentist. The guide says "Tidak ada pendaftaran peserta ke Dokter Gigi lain" (no registration with another dentist). Its FAQ (p. 21) asks whether a participant whose puskesmas has no dentist can pick a dokter gigi praktik mandiri instead. The answer is **"Tidak bisa"** (no): the puskesmas or klinik must provide a jejaring dentist, and if it has none, the participant is referred to FKRTL.
- **Registered at a dokter praktik perorangan (GP):** the participant **chooses and registers a separate dokter gigi praktik mandiri** by filling in the DIP form. Mobile JKN shows this as "fasilitas kesehatan gigi". The participant can only use that chosen dentist.
- **Outside the registered area, or in an emergency:** Perpres 82/2018 Pasal 55 ayat (2)-(3), as amended, allows first-level outpatient care at another FKTP for up to **3 visits within 1 month, at the same FKTP**. A medical emergency can be treated at any facility.
- **Changing FKTP:** Perpres 59/2024 Pasal 7 ayat (1) allows a change **after 3 months** registered. Earlier is allowed only for a move of residence or official duty/training, with a supporting letter. The change takes effect on the **1st of the following month** (ayat 3). The BPJS guide applies the same 3-month minimum to changing a dokter gigi. Changes can be made in Mobile JKN or through Care Center 165.

**Implication for a "BPJS-aware nearby shortlist":** "Accepts BPJS" does not mean the user can be seen there for free. The right model is:
1. The user's own registered FKTP, or its dentist or jejaring dentist. This is usable now.
2. Other BPJS-partner dentists nearby. These are only options to **switch to**: the user must have been registered for 3 or more months, the change applies from next month, and it is only possible for a participant registered at a GP practice or for a full FKTP change.
3. Everything else is out-of-pocket.

A shortlist still helps someone choosing or switching FKTP, but it must not suggest "walk in with your BPJS card".

**Confidence:** **confirmed** (BPJS guide pp. 10-11, 21; Perpres 59/2024 Pasal 7; Perpres 82/2018 Pasal 55). The guide is older than Perpres 59/2024, so whether today's jejaring rules still match it in detail is **likely**.

## 3. Public STR lookup (SATUSEHAT SDMK / KKI)

**Answer**
- **Public profile pages exist** at `https://satusehat.kemkes.go.id/sdmk/nakes/<STR number>`. They are indexed by search engines, need no login, and showed no captcha. They are keyed by **STR number**. Each page has the heading "Berikut hasil pengecekan profil SDMK" and shows:
  - No. STR plus status (e.g., "Tervalidasi")
  - Nama, Jenis kelamin, Kompetensi
  - SKP sufficiency status and period
  - One block per **SIP**: No. SIP, issue date, expiry date, tempat praktik, provinsi, kab/kota
  - "Sumber data SATUSEHAT SDMK terakhir diperbarui" (last-updated line)
- **Search by name: not found.** The SDMK landing page has no public search form. It offers register/login only, and its "Panduan" section is marked "Segera Hadir". I found no public name search on kki.go.id either (unconfirmed).
- **No terms of use** are shown on the profile page. Since UU 17/2023, the STR is valid for life and the SIP every 5 years.
- **Note:** the SDMK homepage says registration for medical and health workers goes exclusively through SATUSEHAT SDMK from 15 Oct 2026.

**Confidence:** profile page and fields **confirmed**. No name search **likely**. Terms and rate limits **unconfirmed**.

**Sources:** https://satusehat.kemkes.go.id/sdmk ; example profile: https://satusehat.kemkes.go.id/sdmk/nakes/RA00000389817621 ; Kemenkes: https://kemkes.go.id/id/urus-str-makin-mudah-cepat-dan-transparan

## 4. Fasyankes registries and open datasets (Surabaya klinik / praktik dokter gigi)

**Answer**
- **registrasifasyankes.kemkes.go.id and dfo.kemkes.go.id are for facility users.** Facilities log in to register and update their data, and receive a kode fasyankes under KMK HK.01.07/MENKES/223/2022. The DFO juknis (Ditjen Yankes, 2022) says the results are **published on yankes.kemkes.go.id** as public information: "data profile fasyankes, SDM, pelayanan serta posisi fasyankes dalam google maps". The covered types include **Praktik Mandiri Dokter Gigi**. Each facility certificate carries a QR code linking to its public profile.
- **Public search found:** `keslan.kemkes.go.id/klinik` has filters for province, kab/kota, type (Pratama/Utama) and clinic name. No results were returned in my fetch (it is JS-rendered), so the result fields are unconfirmed. Indexed praktik mandiri profile pages on `keslan.kemkes.go.id/praktekmandiri/profile/...` showed category, address, provinsi, kab/kota and schedule. Those URLs now return 404, so availability is unstable. I found no public praktik mandiri search page.
- **Open datasets:**
  - satudata.kemkes.go.id now 301-redirects to satusehat.kemkes.go.id/dashboard. I found no facility-list dataset there.
  - **Surabaya's CKAN (ckan.surabaya.go.id, Dinas Kesehatan org)** has only **aggregate counts**, e.g., "Banyaknya Fasilitas Kesehatan menurut Jenisnya per Kecamatan" (2023/2024), plus a **puskesmas list** (kode, name, address, kecamatan, wilayah kerja).
  - I found **no named list of klinik or praktik dokter gigi**. License fields are empty ("No License Provided").

**Confidence:** DFO publication intent **confirmed** (juknis). keslan search fields **unconfirmed**. Surabaya datasets **confirmed** (CKAN API listing).

**Sources:** https://dfo.kemkes.go.id/assets/Juknis/PETUNJUK%20TEKNIS%20DFO%201.pdf (pp. 1-2, 38) ; https://registrasifasyankes.kemkes.go.id/assets/Juknis%20Registrasi%20Tempat%20Praktik%20Mandiri%20Nakes.pdf ; https://keslan.kemkes.go.id/klinik ; https://ckan.surabaya.go.id/api/3/action/package_search?q=klinik ; https://opendata.surabaya.go.id/

## 7. Google Places API (New): storage and attribution

**Answer**
- **Place ID:** "You can therefore store place ID values indefinitely." Place ID is exempt from the caching restrictions. Do not pre-fetch, cache or store any other content beyond the stated exceptions.
- **Lat/lng:** the Service Specific Terms (Places API section) let you "temporarily cache latitude (lat) and longitude (lng) values... for up to 30 consecutive calendar days", then delete them. Confirmed in the 2025-03-31 and 2025-05-01 archived versions. I could not render the current version, so treat this as likely unchanged.
- **Without a Google Map:** "Places API results displayed on a map must be shown on a Google Map." Showing data **without a map is allowed**, but "you must include the Google logo":
  - logo 16-19dp tall, with 10dp clear space left, right and top, and 5dp at the bottom
  - if there is no room, plain text "**Google Maps**", not localized
  - for third-party data, the text must name "Google Maps" plus the provider
- **Reviews:** always credit the author, with avatar, name and profile link when space allows. If space is tight, the avatar is the minimum. Users must be able to open the source review on Google Maps via `googleMapsUri`. Recommended: explain how reviews are ordered and filtered, and mark translated reviews.
- **Photos:** same author-credit rule. In thumbnails or galleries, attribution can be omitted only if the enlarged view shows it. Link via `googleMapsUri`.
- The policies page was last updated 2026-10-15 UTC.

**Confidence:** **confirmed** (policies page). Lat/lng 30-day rule **likely current**.

**Sources:** https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies ; https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms ; archive: https://cloud.google.com/archive/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms-20250501

## 9. Kepmenkes HK.01.07/MENKES/1186/2022: correction and availability

**Answer**
- **1186/2022 is not the PPK for dentists.** Its title is *Panduan Praktik Klinis bagi **Dokter** di Fasilitas Pelayanan Kesehatan Tingkat Pertama*, i.e., for physicians at FKTP. It replaced KMK 514/2015 and was amended by KMK HK.01.07/MENKES/1936/2022.
- **The dentist PPK is KMK HK.02.02/MENKES/62/2015**, *Panduan Praktik Klinis bagi Dokter Gigi*. It is listed on regulasi.bkpk.kemkes.go.id, which did not resolve from here.
- **Newer dental PNPK:** KMK HK.01.07/MENKES/1492/2023 (pulp/periradicular disease) is downloadable from jdih.kemkes.go.id. Confirmed: the PDF opened and carries the jdih footer.
- **1186/2022 availability:** I found no working jdih.kemkes.go.id URL. Copies are on mirrors such as manuver.tireg7.net and doku.pub. The repository.kemkes.go.id/book/755 entry links to a Drive file that turned out to be a different book (Panduan Pelayanan Kesehatan Bergerak).
- **Licensing:**
  - Under UU 28/2014 Pasal 42, statutory regulations and official government decisions are **not protected by copyright**, so the decree text can be reused (likely; I did not re-read the law).
  - The repository.kemkes.go.id catalog page says "© Copyright 2022. All Rights Reserved by Kemenkes". That notice covers the portal. Attached guideline text written by professional bodies (PDGI/PB IDI) may be argued separately.

**Confidence:** title correction **confirmed**. Official URL for 1186 **unconfirmed**. Licensing **likely**.

## 10. Dental emergency red flags

**Answer**
- **PNPK 1492/2023** (Indonesian, official):
  - It describes acute apical abscess with systemic signs (fever, high white-cell count) that can spread into **cellulitis** (p. 46).
  - It says drainage should start as early as possible (p. 47).
  - Table 3.4 (p. 53, after AAE 2019) lists signs that call for adjunct antibiotics:
    - temperature >37.8°C, malaise
    - **trismus**, lymphadenopathy
    - swelling that develops quickly (<24 h)
    - **cellulitis or a spreading infection involving the face**
    - osteomyelitis
  - It does **not** contain explicit "send to IGD" wording or mention Ludwig's angina (I searched the full text).
- **I did not review PPK 62/2015 (dentist) or 1186/2022 (physician) for this.** The 1186 mirror's body is image-only.
- **WHO:** I found no WHO document with explicit emergency-referral criteria (unconfirmed).
- **Non-Indonesian clinical guidance agrees on immediate emergency or specialist referral for:**
  - facial or neck swelling
  - **difficulty swallowing or breathing**
  - trismus (VCH uses interincisal opening <25 mm)
  - fever or signs of sepsis
  - rapid progression
  - immunocompromise
  - Ludwig's angina or floor-of-mouth swelling, which is an airway emergency
  
  Examples: VCH Canada, the UK Oral Health Foundation, and Metro North Queensland.
- **Practical rule for the product:** if the user reports facial swelling plus fever, trismus, dysphagia/dyspnoea, or swelling under the tongue or jaw, show "go to IGD now" and do not show a clinic shortlist.

**Confidence:** PNPK content **confirmed**. "Immediate IGD" wording **likely**, based on international guidance rather than an Indonesian PPK.
