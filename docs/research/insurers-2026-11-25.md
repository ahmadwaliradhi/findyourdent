## TPAs: hints, not entries

**Decision: AdMedika, Owlexa and Fullerton Health are not insurer entries.** The picker shows a hint instead, for example "Kartu AdMedika atau Owlexa? Pilih asuransi yang tertera di kartu."

Reasons:

- A TPA (third-party administrator) runs cashless claims and hospital networks for insurers; it does not carry the risk or issue the policy. AdMedika works with many insurers (13 sit on its Medical Advisory Board) and Owlexa serves insurers such as Takaful Keluarga. OJK's POJK 36/2025 lists TPAs as a party an insurer may work with, not as an insurer.
- The same insurer (Great Eastern, for example) uses AdMedika for some products and others for the rest. A Patient who picks "AdMedika" would split that insurer's count and say nothing about which insurer they hold.
- Ownership is moving: Fullerton Health completed its acquisition of AdMedika on 3 Jun 2026, so a TPA entry would also go stale.
- Owlexa is a Lintasarta unit; I found no 2026 news specific to it, so the hint wording carries no claim about it beyond "card brand".
