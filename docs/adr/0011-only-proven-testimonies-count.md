# Only Proven Testimonies count, and sign-in stays Google only

Dentists told us that clinics pay buzzers for good reviews on Google Maps and social media. A Google account is free to create in bulk, so neither one Testimony per account nor Gemini Moderation stops a campaign: each fake Testimony looks clean on its own. We considered phone or WhatsApp OTP and rejected it, now and later: buzzers buy registered SIM cards cheaply, it adds sign-up friction and messaging cost, and it adds phone numbers as personal data under UU 27/2022. We considered a code issued by the clinic and rejected it, because the clinic would gate its own Testimonies.

Instead only a Proven Testimony (one with an accepted Proof of Visit) feeds aspect averages, directory and Shortlist ranking, and price stats. Testimonies without proof are still published under the same Moderation rules, labelled "Belum terbukti", so honest Patients without a receipt are not silenced but cannot be outnumbered by buzzers. A Provider needs at least three Proven Testimonies before a star average is shown. Proof is checked against the Provider's name, the visit month (±1 month) and a 12-month age, and a Proof Fingerprint (never the receipt) stops one receipt being reused. Existing Testimonies are not grandfathered. Spec 09 has the details.

## Consequences

- Credibility over volume: on launch most Providers show "Belum cukup testimoni terbukti". We accept the empty state.
- A fake Testimony that counts now costs a real visit, not a few thousand rupiah.
- A doubtful proof goes to an admin and its file is kept up to 7 days for that, amending spec 03's immediate deletion; the Testimony itself stays published as "Belum terbukti".
- Phone and email verification of authors is permanently out of scope; revisit only with new evidence of account farming that proof cannot stop.
