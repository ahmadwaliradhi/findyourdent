  1. Sign in with your Google account.

  2. Upload a photo or PDF of your SIP or practice permit. It is kept private and deleted after the decision.

  3. The FindYourDent team checks the document and phones the number on this place's Google Maps listing, within [n] working days.

In line with the dental code of ethics, there is no promotion, advertising, discounts or paid ranking. A claim does not change the Shortlist order, and practices cannot delete or hide Testimonies.
