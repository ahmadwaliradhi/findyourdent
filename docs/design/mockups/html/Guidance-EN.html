Your complaint

My lower left molar has been throbbing for three days and hurts more at night.

Guidance for your problem

Urgency

SoonSee a dentist within 1 to 3 days
RoutineScheduledSoonEmergency

Likely problem

Deep cavity or inflamed tooth nerve

A throbbing pain that gets worse at night often shows up when a cavity has reached close to the nerve. A dentist needs to examine the tooth to confirm it.

Expected visits

Usually needs several visits

  1. Visit 1Examination and dental X-ray, then the root canal is opened or the tooth gets a temporary filling.
  2. Visit 2The root canal is cleaned and medicated.
  3. Visit 3A permanent filling or a crown.

Likely procedures

Root canal treatmentFillingTooth extraction
