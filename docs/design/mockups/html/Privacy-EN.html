1. Who we are

FindYourDent is a dentistry platform for Indonesia operated by [legal entity name]. This policy explains what data we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights.

For privacy questions, contact us at business@findyourdent.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Your dental complaint, typed or as a voice note, is used to write your Guidance. It is not stored and is never logged.
  • Your location or area is used only to find dentists near you and is not stored. In the Directory, distance is computed on your device and your location is not sent to us.
  • Your Google account is used when you write a Testimony or claim a page. Your account is never shown.
  • Testimonies: ratings, story, month and year of visit, the name you choose, and the procedure, price and photos if you add them. These are public. Location data in photos is removed automatically.
  • Proof of visit is checked and then deleted right away. It is never shown; only a Verified visit mark appears if it matches.
  • Owner claim data: your SIP or practice permit is kept private and deleted after the decision. Your phone number is only for the FindYourDent team and is never shown.
  • Owner price lists: the original file is kept privately for 30 days in case of disputes, then deleted. Only rows the owner confirms are shown.
  • Reports on a Testimony: the category and details you write, for the team to review.

3. Cookies

We use one cookie, __session, to remember your language, keep you signed in, and keep your Guidance session. It is kept for up to a year if you choose a language or sign in with Google.

We do not set advertising cookies.

4. Security and abuse prevention

We use App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to prevent abuse. To limit request rates, we store a random value that cannot be turned back into your IP address, token or session ID.

5. AI and service providers

  • Your complaint is processed by Google AI (Gemini on Vertex AI) outside Indonesia.
  • Practice details, opening hours, Google ratings and reviews come from Google Maps.
  • Sign-in uses Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Testimonies, photos and account data are stored on Google Cloud in Jakarta.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. How long we keep data

  • Complaints and location: not stored.
  • Proof of visit: deleted right after it is checked.
  • Claim documents: deleted after the decision.
  • Original price list files: 30 days.
  • Testimonies: while published, until you or the team delete them.
  • The __session cookie: up to one year.

7. Your rights

You can edit or delete a Testimony at any time from My Testimonies. Deleted text and photos are gone permanently.

To ask for a copy, correction or deletion of other data about you, email business@findyourdent.com. We will reply within [time period].

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page. We will show important changes on the site before they take effect.
