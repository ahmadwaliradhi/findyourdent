About us

Understand your dental problem, then choose a dentist with confidence.

FindYourDent is a dentistry platform for Indonesia. We help Patients understand their dental problem and choose a credible, affordable dentist, and we give dentists and clinics a public identity.

Built for the AI Builder Cup 2026.

What we do

  • Guidance for your problem

    Describe your problem in your own words, typed or as a voice note. We explain how urgent it is, what it may be, the visits it usually takes, why prices vary, and BPJS coverage.

  • Straight to the IGD when danger signs appear

    When danger signs appear, such as facial swelling with fever or difficulty swallowing, we send you to the nearest hospital emergency department first, ahead of anything else.

  • Dentists near you that fit

    A list of dental clinics, private dentists and hospitals near you, with Patients’ Testimonies and price signals.

  • Testimonies from real patients

    Patients write about their visit: service, hygiene, materials and equipment, and the price they paid.

How we keep you safe

Gemini helps with the work that needs judgement. Anything that must never be wrong is handled by fixed rules and curated data.

  • Danger signs are checked first

    The danger-sign check runs before any AI call, and the AI can never lower its urgency.

  • Costs and BPJS from official sources

    Cost estimates, BPJS coverage and safety rules come from knowledge files that cite their regulation, not from the AI model.

  • Your complaint is not stored

    Neither typed complaints nor voice notes are stored. A shared guidance link contains only the guidance, never your complaint.

  • People make the decisions

    The AI publishes clean Testimonies and holds doubtful ones. The FindYourDent team decides on every held or reported Testimony. The AI never deletes.

From Indonesia, for the world

We start in Indonesia because it is where we best understand Patients, dentists and the care system, including BPJS. Going forward, we plan to expand FindYourDent worldwide in stages, starting with the Asia Pacific region.

  1. Now

    Indonesia

    Guidance, emergency (IGD) escalation, the Directory and Testimonies for Patients across Indonesia, in Indonesian and English.

  2. Next stage

    Asia Pacific

    A step-by-step expansion into Asia Pacific countries, adapted to each country’s languages, health care system and rules.

  3. Further ahead

    Worldwide

    Bringing the same approach to more countries, one at a time.

Each new country opens only once its cost guidance, coverage information and safety rules are built from that country’s official sources.

Our principles

  1. Educational and navigation information, not a diagnosis or a prescription.

  2. No promotion, advertising, discounts or paid ranking. Dentists are never ranked by payment.

  3. Practices can reply to or report a Testimony, but cannot delete or hide it.
