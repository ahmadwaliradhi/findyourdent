1. Accepting these terms

By using FindYourDent, you agree to these terms of use and our Privacy policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2. Not a medical service

FindYourDent gives educational and navigation information, not a diagnosis or a prescription. Guidance does not replace an examination by a dentist.

Cost estimates are a general picture, not a price quote from any practice.

3. Emergencies

If you have danger signs, such as swelling of the face or neck, fever, difficulty swallowing or breathing, or bleeding that does not stop, go to the nearest hospital emergency department (IGD) now. Do not wait for a dentist appointment.

FindYourDent does not monitor your condition and does not contact emergency services for you.

4. Accounts

You sign in with a Google account when you write a Testimony or claim a page. You are responsible for activity on your account. Each Patient can write one Testimony per practice.

5. Rules for writing a Testimony

A Testimony must be:

  • your own experience, written honestly;
  • free of other people’s personal data, such as phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers, or the full names of patients or staff;
  • free of accusations stated as fact and of harassment;
  • not an advert, link, promo code, or someone else’s story;
  • not medical advice for readers, such as medicine names and doses;
  • about the right practice.

Photos may only show the waiting room, treatment room, equipment or signboard, with no faces, receipts or screenshots.

6. Moderation

Every Testimony is screened. The AI publishes clean Testimonies, asks you to fix specific parts, or holds them for review. The FindYourDent team decides on every held or reported Testimony, usually within 24 hours, and may take it down if it breaks the rules. The AI never deletes a Testimony.

You can edit or delete your Testimony at any time.

7. For owners and dentists

  • Claiming a page needs a SIP or practice permit and a phone check by the FindYourDent team.
  • Practice details and replies are checked before they are shown.
  • In line with the dental code of ethics, there is no promotion, advertising, discounts or paid ranking. A claim does not change how dentists are ranked.
  • Practices can reply to or report a Testimony, but cannot delete or hide it.
  • Owners are responsible for the accuracy of the price lists they confirm.

8. Information from others

Ratings, reviews, opening hours and place details from Google Maps are shown as they are, with their source. FindYourDent does not change or guarantee that information.
